/poppenspeler means puppeteer in the Dutch language/

🚧 work-in-progress

poppenspeler simplifies end-to-end testing by providing a powerful orchestration tool and expressive language for your applications and their dependencies.

Define your test scenarios in a DSL (YAML, TOML or JSON) and automate the spinning up (and down) of complex environments involving multiple programs, databases, and services, executing comprehensive tests with ease.

poppenspeler is built with Rust, enhancing safety, performance and reliability, with a modern and accessible code-base.
